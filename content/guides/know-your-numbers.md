## The six

| Number | Healthy range for most adults | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blood pressure | Under 120/80 mm Hg | Home monitor or pharmacy |
| LDL cholesterol | Under 100 mg/dL (2.6 mmol/L) | Blood test |
| Blood sugar (A1C) | Under 5.7% | Blood test |
| Waist-to-height ratio | Under 0.5 | Tape measure |
| Resting heart rate | 60 to 100 beats a minute | Fingers and a clock |
| Body mass index | 18.5 to 24.9 | Scale and height |

Targets differ if you already have heart disease, diabetes or kidney disease. Your doctor will set those.

## Blood pressure

High blood pressure rarely causes symptoms and is a leading cause of heart attack, stroke and kidney disease. Check it at least once a year. [What your reading means](/tools/blood-pressure-checker.html).

## Cholesterol

A lipid panel reports four values:

- **LDL** ("bad"): lower is better. Under 100 mg/dL is optimal for most adults.
- **HDL** ("good"): 40 mg/dL or more for men, 50 or more for women.
- **Triglycerides:** under 150 mg/dL (1.7 mmol/L).
- **Total:** under 200 mg/dL (5.2 mmol/L).

Doctors now look less at any single value and more at your overall 10-year risk, which also counts age, blood pressure, smoking and diabetes.

## Blood sugar

A1C reflects average glucose over three months. Under 5.7% is normal, 5.7 to 6.4% is prediabetes and 6.5% or higher suggests diabetes. Prediabetes is common, usually silent, and often reversible with modest weight loss and activity. [Convert an A1C](/tools/a1c-converter.html).

## Waist and weight

Your waist should be less than half your height. This catches risky abdominal fat that BMI misses. Use both: [waist-to-height ratio](/tools/waist-to-height-ratio.html) and [BMI](/tools/bmi-calculator.html).

## Resting heart rate

Count your pulse for 60 seconds after sitting quietly for five minutes. Lower generally reflects better fitness; many active people sit in the 50s. A resting rate that is consistently over 100, or a rhythm that feels irregular, deserves a check.

## Keep a record

Write your numbers down with the date, or use [My numbers](/my-numbers.html), which stores them in your browser. Bring the list to appointments. The [know-your-numbers week](/challenges/7-day-know-your-numbers-week.html) walks you through collecting them one day at a time.
