Keep Healthy.Life free for everyone

No account, no paywall, no selling of personal data. If the tools helped you, you can help the next person find them.

Where support goes

  • Running the siteDomain, hosting, monitoring and the automated updates that keep news, videos and tools current.
  • New toolsDesign, testing and source-checking for each calculator and challenge.
  • Expert reviewPaying dietitians, nurses, doctors and exercise professionals to review content.
  • PeopleHiring writers, reviewers, translators and developers. See open roles.
  • ReachPromotion and marketing so the tools reach people who would not otherwise find them.
  • Contests and prizesFunding the prizes in our monthly contest.

Other ways to help

  • Share a tool with someone who would use it.
  • Tell us what is unclear or wrong through the contact form.
  • Sponsor a tool or a challenge. Sponsorship options.
  • Lend your expertise. Work with us.

Healthy.Life is an independent publication, not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible. Supporters receive no influence over content.
