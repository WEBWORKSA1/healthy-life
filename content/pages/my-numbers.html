Reading the page

Each tile links to the full tool, where you will find the formula, its limits and its sources. Results from tools that need extra measurements, such as blood pressure, A1C or body fat, appear under "Saved from other tools" after you use them.

These are screening estimates for adults. They are not a diagnosis, and they cannot account for pregnancy, medical conditions or medicines.
