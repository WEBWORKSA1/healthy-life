About Healthy.Life

A free toolkit that turns public-health guidelines into numbers you can use and small steps you can take.

Why this site exists

Good health advice is public, free and mostly agreed. It is also scattered across hundreds of pages written for professionals. Calculator sites give you a number and leave. Health magazines give you articles and no way to apply them. Programmes that do both usually want a subscription.

Healthy.Life puts the pieces in one place. You enter your details once. Every tool uses them. A short lifestyle check shows which area deserves your attention, and a 30-day plan turns that into action. All of it is free and none of it needs an account.

What you will find

  • Calculators for body measures, energy and nutrition, movement, sleep, and heart and metabolic health.
  • The Healthy Life Score, an 18-question self-check across six pillars.
  • A free 30-day plan built from your answers.
  • Challenges with daily trackers and certificates.
  • Guides that start with the answer and show their sources.
  • Health Pulse and videos, refreshed automatically from trusted sources.

Who writes it

Content is produced by the Healthy.Life editorial team: writers and developers who work from guidelines and evidence reviews published by recognised health bodies. We are not a medical practice, and unless a page names a credentialed reviewer, it has not been reviewed by a clinician. We are recruiting registered dietitians, nurses, doctors and exercise professionals as paid reviewers; see work with us.

How we choose sources, handle corrections and keep advertising separate is set out in our editorial policy.

What we believe

  • Numbers need context. A result means little without the range it sits in and the limits of the formula.
  • Small beats heroic. The changes that last are the ones that fit into an ordinary Tuesday.
  • Privacy is a feature. Your measurements stay in your browser.
  • Health is more than weight. Sleep, stress, relationships and prevention sit beside movement and food on purpose.
  • Honesty about uncertainty. Where experts disagree, we say so.

How the site is funded

Healthy.Life earns money from advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorship, fees from professionals and programmes we introduce to visitors who ask for an introduction, and reader support. None of these affects what a calculator outputs or what a guide says. Details are in the funding section of our editorial policy.

Independence
