Privacy and cookies

The short version: what you type into our tools stays in your browser. We receive personal details only when you send a form.

Last updated 19 November 2026.

At a glance

WhatWhere it goes
Numbers you enter in calculators, your profile, your score answers, challenge progressStored in your own browser only. Never sent to us.

Who we are

Healthy.Life is an independent website. "We" means its publisher. You can reach us through the contact form; choose "Privacy request".

Information kept in your browser

To save you retyping, the site stores some information in your browser's local storage: your profile (units, sex, age, height, weight and optional measurements), results you save, your Healthy Life Score and its history, plan answers, challenge progress, your light or dark theme, and whether you have dismissed notices. This information stays on your device. We cannot see it. Clear it at any time with the button on My numbers or through your browser settings.

If you copy a "link to this result", the figures are placed in that link. Anyone you share the link with can see them.

Information you send us

When you submit a form we receive what you typed, the page you sent it from, the site that referred you if any, and the time. Forms are transmitted through a third-party form-delivery service (FormSubmit) that forwards them to our email inbox; it processes the submission, including your IP address, to deliver it and to prevent spam.

We use this information to:

  • answer your message or application;
  • provide the plan you asked for;
  • introduce you to professionals or programmes, only if you ticked the box asking for introductions. In that case we share your first name, contact details, goals, focus pillars, preferred format and budget band with up to three matched providers, who may contact you. We may receive a fee from them;
  • run a contest you entered and contact winners;
  • arrange a contribution you pledged;
  • send the emails you signed up for.

We do not ask for medical records, diagnoses or medicines, and we ask you not to include them in messages. We do not sell personal information.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, to limit how often you see an ad, and to measure performance. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and others on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message from Google's certified consent platform before advertising cookies are used, where that message is enabled. You can change your choice at any time through the privacy link that message provides.

Healthy.Life itself sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics scripts of its own.

Videos and external links

Video thumbnails are images loaded from YouTube. A video player, and YouTube's cookies, load only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain. Links to other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host's servers log technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and operation. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

How long we keep information

Form submissions are kept in our inbox for as long as needed to deal with the request, and for up to 24 months afterwards for records, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Email sign-ups are kept until you unsubscribe. Contest entries are kept for 12 months after the round closes.

Legal bases

Where laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on your consent (forms, introductions, email sign-ups, advertising cookies), on performing what you asked for (plans, contests, applications) and on our legitimate interests in running, securing and funding the site.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25 and U.S. state laws such as the CCPA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to withdraw consent, to object to certain uses, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Send a request through the contact form. We respond within 30 days. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the advertising described above, which you can control with the opt-outs listed.

Children

Healthy.Life is written for adults. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, tell us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
