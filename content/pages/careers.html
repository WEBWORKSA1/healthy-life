Work with us

We are building a bench of people who can explain health clearly and check that it is right. Work is freelance, remote and paid per piece or per review.

Who we are looking for

  • Expert reviewersRegistered dietitians, nurses, physicians, pharmacists, physiotherapists and certified exercise professionals who review guides and tools against current guidelines. Current registration required.
  • Health writersWriters who can turn a guideline into 600 clear words, cite every claim and avoid hype.
  • Video creatorsQualified instructors for short exercise, cooking and breathing videos.
  • TranslatorsNative-speaker translators with health-content experience, starting with French, Spanish and Hindi.
  • Developers and designersPeople who enjoy small, fast, accessible tools built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • PartnershipsSomeone to bring on sponsors and professional partners, paid on results.
  • Community moderatorsReaders and judges for contest entries and reader stories.

How it works

  1. Send the form with a link to your work or your registration details.
  2. If there is a fit, we reply with a short paid trial piece.
  3. Regular contributors are credited on the work they write or review.

Roles open as funding allows; reader support and sponsorship pay for them. We reply to every application that includes a work sample.
