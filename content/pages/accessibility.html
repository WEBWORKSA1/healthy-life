Accessibility

Health information should work for everyone. This page explains what we have done and where we still fall short.

What we aim for

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

How the site is built

  • Body text is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a typeface designed with the Braille Institute for low-vision readers.
  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, has a "skip to content" link and shows a clear focus outline.
  • Form fields have visible labels, errors are announced, and results update in regions that screen readers announce.
  • Gauges and charts carry text descriptions, and colour is never the only way information is shown.
  • Light and dark themes are both available, and text can be zoomed to 200% without loss of content.
  • Animation is limited to responses to your actions and is switched off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Pages are light and work on slow connections; tools you have opened keep working offline.

Known limitations

  • Embedded videos are third-party content; captions depend on the creator.
  • Advertisements are supplied by a third party and may not meet the same standard.
  • Downloadable certificates and score cards are images; the same information is shown as text on the page.
  • The breathing timer relies on a moving circle; the phase is also written as text and announced.

Tell us about a barrier

If something does not work for you, please use the contact form and tell us the page, what you were trying to do and the technology you use. We aim to reply within two working days and to fix problems quickly.
