How these tools are built

  • Published formulas only. Each tool names its equation and links to the source, so you can check our working.
  • Ranges before verdicts. Results sit on a scale with the guideline bands marked. A number without its range tells you little.
  • Guard rails. Calorie tools never suggest less than 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men), and weight goals under a BMI of 18.5 are not accepted.
  • Private by design. Calculations run in your browser. We do not receive or store what you type.

Tools estimate; they do not diagnose. Use them to prepare better questions for a health professional.
