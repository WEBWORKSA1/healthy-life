Official rules

  1. Organiser. The contest is run by the publisher of Healthy.Life ("we"). It is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by any social network, search engine or advertising partner.
  2. No purchase necessary. Entry is free. A purchase, payment or contribution does not improve the chance of winning.
  3. Eligibility. Open to individuals who have reached the age of majority in their place of residence. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. People involved in running the contest and members of their households may not enter.
  4. Entry period. Each round opens when announced on this page and closes at the date and time shown above. Late entries are not considered.
  5. How to enter. Complete a Healthy.Life challenge and submit the form on this page. One entry per person per round. Entries must be your own original work, truthful, and free of medical claims, advertising and other people's personal details.
  6. Judging. This is a contest of skill. Entries are scored by the Healthy.Life editorial team on the criteria and weightings listed above. The highest score wins; ties are broken on the honesty and clarity score. Decisions are final.
  7. Prizes. The prizes are those shown on this page for the round. Prizes are delivered digitally where possible, are not transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash unless we choose to offer it. If a stated prize is unavailable in the winner's country, we will substitute one of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  8. Skill-testing question. Residents of Canada must correctly answer the mathematical skill-testing question on the entry form to be declared a winner.
  9. Notification. Winners are contacted by email within 14 days of the closing date and have 14 days to reply. If there is no reply, or the winner is ineligible, the next highest-scoring entry is selected.
  10. Publicity. We publish a winner's first name and region. Stories are published only with the entrant's permission and may be edited for length.
  11. Personal information. Entry details are used to run the contest and are handled under our privacy policy.
  12. Health and safety. Challenges are general wellbeing activities. Take part within your own limits and seek medical advice where appropriate. Entries describing extreme dieting, unsafe exercise or rapid weight loss will be disqualified.
  13. General. We may cancel, suspend or change a round if fraud, technical failure or events outside our control affect it; if so, a notice will appear on this page. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for lost, late or incomplete entries. These rules are governed by the law of the organiser's place of establishment, without limiting any rights you have under the mandatory law of the place where you live.
