## The conversion

Estimated average glucose (mg/dL) = 28.7 × A1C − 46.7

To get mmol/L, divide by 18. The formula comes from a study that matched A1C results with months of continuous glucose readings.

| A1C | Average glucose | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5% | 97 mg/dL | 5.4 mmol/L |
| 6% | 126 mg/dL | 7.0 mmol/L |
| 7% | 154 mg/dL | 8.6 mmol/L |
| 8% | 183 mg/dL | 10.2 mmol/L |
| 9% | 212 mg/dL | 11.8 mmol/L |

## The ranges

| A1C | Range |
| --- | --- |
| Under 5.7% | Normal |
| 5.7 to 6.4% | Prediabetes |
| 6.5% or higher | Diabetes |

A diagnosis needs a second confirming test and a doctor's judgement. If you already have diabetes, many adults are given a target under 7%, but targets are personal.

## Prediabetes is a fork in the road

Most people with prediabetes do not know they have it. Losing 5 to 7% of body weight and reaching 150 active minutes a week more than halved the rate of progression to type 2 diabetes in a landmark U.S. trial.
