## The method

The U.S. Navy formula predicts body fat from circumferences and height. It was developed on service personnel and checked against underwater weighing.

- Men: uses waist minus neck, and height
- Women: uses waist plus hips minus neck, and height

It works because waist and hip size rise with fat, while neck size tracks lean mass.

## How to measure

- **Neck:** just below the Adam's apple, tape sloping slightly down at the front.
- **Waist:** men at the navel; women at the narrowest point. Breathe out normally.
- **Hips (women):** around the widest part of the buttocks.

Take each measurement twice and use the average.

## Reading the result

| Category (ACE chart) | Women | Men |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essential fat | 10–13% | 2–5% |
| Athletes | 14–20% | 6–13% |
| Fitness | 21–24% | 14–17% |
| Average | 25–31% | 18–24% |
| Above average | 32% and over | 25% and over |

Body fat naturally rises with age, and women need more of it than men for normal hormone function. Very low levels are not a health goal.
