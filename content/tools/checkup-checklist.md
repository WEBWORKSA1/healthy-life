## Why a list helps

Appointments are short. People who arrive with a list get more of the checks that find problems early, when they are easiest to treat. High blood pressure, high cholesterol, prediabetes and several cancers usually cause no symptoms at first.

## Where the list comes from

Items follow the A and B grade recommendations of the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force, an independent panel that reviews the evidence for each test. They apply to people at average risk with no symptoms.

## Your list may differ

- **Family history:** a close relative with bowel, breast or ovarian cancer, early heart disease or diabetes can mean starting earlier.
- **Where you live:** national programmes set their own ages and intervals. The NHS, Canadian provinces and Australian programmes differ from U.S. advice in places.
- **Your own health:** existing conditions change what is needed and when.

## Making the most of the visit

Bring your list, your home blood pressure readings if you have them, the names of your medicines and supplements, and your top two concerns. Ask what each result means and when to come back. For an official personalised list in the U.S., use MyHealthfinder.
