## Where the range comes from

The range is the set of weights that give a body mass index between 18.5 and 24.9 at your height: the band that the World Health Organization and the CDC class as healthy for adults.

**Example:** at 170 cm, BMI 18.5 is 53.5 kg and BMI 24.9 is 72 kg. At 5 ft 7 in, that is about 118 to 159 lb.

## Why it is a range, not a number

Formulas that give one "ideal weight" were mostly designed to calculate drug doses, not to tell anyone what they should weigh. A span of roughly 18 kg (40 lb) is honest about how much healthy bodies differ in frame and muscle.

## If you are outside it

A few kilos above the range with a waist under half your height, normal blood pressure and regular activity is a different situation from the same weight with a large waist.
