## How it works

Sleep runs in cycles of light, deep and REM sleep, each lasting about 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle, in light sleep, tends to feel easier than being dragged out of deep sleep.

The calculator takes your target time, allows for the minutes you need to fall asleep, and lists the times that fit 6, 5, 4 or 3 full cycles.

**Example:** to wake at 7:00 am with 15 minutes to fall asleep, be in bed by 9:45 pm (6 cycles, 9 hours) or 11:15 pm (5 cycles, 7.5 hours).

## How much you need

| Age | Recommended sleep a night |
| --- | --- |
| 13 to 18 | 8 to 10 hours |
| Adults | 7 or more hours |
| 65 and over | 7 to 8 hours |

These come from the American Academy of Sleep Medicine and the U.S. National Sleep Foundation. Regularly sleeping under 7 hours is linked with higher risk of weight gain, high blood pressure, diabetes, heart disease and low mood.

## The habit that beats any calculator

A fixed wake-up time, seven days a week. It anchors your body clock, and a steady bedtime follows by itself within a week or two.
