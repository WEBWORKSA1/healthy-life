## How it works

First we estimate your resting needs with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Then we multiply by an activity factor:

| Activity level | Factor |
| --- | --- |
| Mostly sitting, little exercise | 1.2 |
| Light: exercise 1 to 3 days a week | 1.375 |
| Moderate: exercise 3 to 5 days a week | 1.55 |
| Very active: hard exercise 6 to 7 days a week | 1.725 |
| Extra active: physical job plus training | 1.9 |

**Example:** resting needs of 1,500 kcal at the "light" level: 1,500 × 1.375 = about 2,060 kcal a day.

## Choosing your level honestly

Activity level is where most estimates go wrong, and nearly always upwards. A desk job plus three gym sessions is usually "light", not "moderate". If you are torn between two, choose the lower and adjust later.

## Using the number

Eat near this figure and your weight should hold steady. Check against reality: if your weight drifts over two to three weeks, your true figure is a little higher or lower. Nudge by 100 to 200 kcal and watch again.
