## How the date is worked out

- **Last period:** first day of your last period + 280 days (40 weeks), adjusted if your cycle is longer or shorter than 28 days. This is Naegele's rule.
- **Conception date:** + 266 days.
- **IVF:** transfer date + 263 days for a day-3 embryo, or + 261 days for a day-5 embryo.

**Example:** last period started 1 March, 28-day cycle: due date 6 December.

## Weeks and trimesters

Pregnancy is counted from the first day of the last period, so you are "2 weeks pregnant" at conception.

| Stage | Weeks |
| --- | --- |
| First trimester | Up to 13 weeks 6 days |
| Second trimester | 14 weeks to 27 weeks 6 days |
| Third trimester | 28 weeks onward |
| Full term | 39 weeks 0 days to 40 weeks 6 days |

## How exact is it?

Only about 4 to 5% of babies are born on their due date; most arrive within two weeks either side. An ultrasound in the first trimester is the most accurate way to date a pregnancy, and your care team will use it if it differs from the period-based date.

## First steps

Book your first antenatal appointment, start folic acid if you have not already, and ask about anything you take regularly.
