## The formulas

- Epley: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30)
- Brzycki: weight × 36 ÷ (37 − reps)

The calculator shows both and uses their average.

**Example:** 60 kg for 5 reps: Epley 70 kg, Brzycki 67.5 kg, estimate about 69 kg.

## Why estimate instead of test?

A true maximum single lift is demanding, needs a spotter and carries more injury risk, especially for beginners. A hard set of 3 to 8 reps gives an estimate that is accurate enough to plan training.

## Using the table

| Goal | Load | Reps a set |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | 80 to 90% | 3 to 6 |
| Muscle size | 65 to 80% | 6 to 12 |
| Muscular endurance | 50 to 65% | 12 to 20 |

For general health, the WHO advises muscle-strengthening activity for all major muscle groups on at least 2 days a week. You do not need heavy barbells: body weight, bands and dumbbells all count, as long as the last few reps of a set feel hard.
