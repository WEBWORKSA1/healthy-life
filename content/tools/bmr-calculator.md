## The formula

This calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which most dietitians treat as the most reliable simple estimate for adults:

- Men: (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) + 5
- Women: (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) − 161

**Example:** a 35-year-old woman, 165 cm, 65 kg: 650 + 1,031 − 175 − 161 = 1,345 kcal a day.

## What BMR is and is not

About 60 to 70% of the energy you use in a day goes on staying alive: heart, lungs, brain, liver, kidneys, and keeping warm. That is your BMR. Movement and digesting food make up the rest.

BMR is **not** a target to eat. Eating at or below it for long is not recommended without medical supervision. For a practical number, multiply by your activity level in the [daily calorie calculator](/tools/tdee-calculator.html).

## What changes it

Bigger and more muscular bodies burn more. BMR falls slowly with age, mostly because muscle is lost, which is one reason strength training matters as you get older.
