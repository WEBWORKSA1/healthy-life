## The guideline

For adults aged 18 and over, including older adults, the World Health Organization recommends each week:

- **150 to 300 minutes** of moderate-intensity aerobic activity, **or**
- **75 to 150 minutes** of vigorous-intensity activity, **or** a mix of the two, **and**
- **muscle-strengthening** activity involving all major muscle groups on **2 or more days**.

Adults over 65 should add balance work on 3 or more days.

## How we count

One vigorous minute counts as two moderate minutes. So 60 moderate plus 45 vigorous gives 60 + 90 = 150 moderate-equivalent minutes.

- **Moderate:** you can talk but not sing. Brisk walking, easy cycling, doubles tennis, mowing the lawn.
- **Vigorous:** a few words at a time. Running, fast cycling, football, hard swimming.

## If you are below 150

Globally, about one adult in three does not reach the guideline, so you have company. The biggest health gain comes from the first step, moving from almost nothing to something. Ten minutes a day is a real start. There is no minimum bout length any more: every minute counts.
