Six pillars, one picture

Health is not one number. Every tool, guide and challenge here belongs to one of six pillars, and your score covers them all.

  • MoveActivity, strength and sitting less
  • NourishEating and drinking well
  • SleepRest, rhythm and recovery
  • MindStress, focus and habits
  • ConnectPeople, purpose and belonging
  • PreventCheckups, numbers and risks

How it works

Three steps, about five minutes, and you can stop after any of them.

  1. Enter your basics onceAge, height and weight unlock most tools. Add waist and resting heart rate for the rest. Open My numbers
  2. Take the 3-minute scoreEighteen questions about movement, food, sleep, stress, people and prevention give you a score out of 100. Take the score
  3. Get a 30-day planWe turn your lowest pillars into four weeks of small steps, with an optional introduction to a vetted professional. Build my plan
