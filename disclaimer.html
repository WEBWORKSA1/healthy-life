Medical disclaimer

Last updated 19 November 2026.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number. Do not use this website to decide whether to seek urgent care. If you are thinking about harming yourself, contact a crisis line or emergency service in your country now.

Education, not medical advice

Everything on Healthy.Life, including its calculators, scores, plans, challenges, guides, news links and videos, is general information for educational purposes. It is not medical advice, and it does not create a doctor-patient or any other professional relationship.

The content is not a substitute for advice, diagnosis or treatment from a qualified health professional who knows your history. Never disregard professional advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you read or calculated here.

What the tools can and cannot do

  • Tools apply published formulas to the figures you enter. Formulas describe averages across groups of people and can be wrong for an individual.
  • Results are estimates for screening and awareness. They cannot diagnose, exclude or monitor any condition.
  • Unless a tool says otherwise, it is designed for non-pregnant adults. Results may not apply to children, teenagers, pregnancy, breastfeeding, older age, athletes, or people with medical conditions or taking medicines.
  • The Healthy Life Score is a self-assessment of habits. It is not a health-risk assessment or a medical device.
  • The 30-day plan and the challenges are general wellbeing suggestions, not a prescription.

Before you change anything

Talk to a health professional before starting or changing an exercise programme, diet, weight-loss plan, supplement or medicine, particularly if you have a heart, lung, kidney, liver or metabolic condition, a history of an eating disorder, are pregnant or breastfeeding, are over 65 and have been inactive, or take prescription medicines. Stop any activity that causes pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or shortness of breath and seek advice.

Eating and body image

Numbers about weight and food can be unhelpful or harmful for some people. If thinking about food, weight or exercise is causing you distress, please skip those tools and speak to a doctor or a specialist support service.

Third-party content

News headlines, videos and external links are provided for convenience. They are produced by third parties, we do not control or verify them, and listing them is not an endorsement. Professionals and programmes we introduce on request are independent; you are responsible for checking that any service suits you.

Accuracy

We work to keep content accurate and current, and we correct errors when we find them. Even so, health knowledge changes and mistakes happen. We make no guarantee that content is complete, current or suitable for your circumstances, and you use it at your own risk, to the extent the law allows. See our terms of use and editorial policy.
