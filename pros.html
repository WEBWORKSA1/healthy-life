For health professionals

People finish a plan on Healthy.Life and some ask for a person to help them follow it. If you are qualified and take new clients, we would like to introduce you.

How introductions work

  1. A visitor completes the 30-day plan and ticks the box asking to be introduced to a professional.
  2. We match on goal, format (in person or online), location, language and budget.
  3. Up to three professionals receive the request: first name, goals, focus pillars, preferred format, budget band and contact details.
  4. You contact the person directly. What you offer and charge is between you and them.

Who can join

  • Registered dietitians and nutritionists with a recognised credential
  • Certified health and wellbeing coaches
  • Certified personal trainers and exercise physiologists
  • Physiotherapists, sleep coaches and stress-management practitioners
  • Structured programmes and clinics with qualified staff

What we ask of you

  • Proof of a current qualification or registration, which we verify.
  • Evidence-based practice: no miracle claims, no pressure selling, no supplements as a condition of service.
  • A reply to each introduction within two working days.
  • Respect for privacy: use contact details only for the request received.

We charge professionals for introductions or a listing. Terms are agreed individually before any introduction is made. Visitors never pay to be matched.
