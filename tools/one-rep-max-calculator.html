One-rep max calculator

Enter a weight and the reps you managed. Get an estimated one-rep max and the loads for common rep ranges.

Your entries stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us.

The formulas

  • Epley: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30)
  • Brzycki: weight × 36 ÷ (37 − reps)

The calculator shows both and uses their average.

Example: 60 kg for 5 reps: Epley 70 kg, Brzycki 67.5 kg, estimate about 69 kg.

Why estimate instead of test?

A true maximum single lift is demanding, needs a spotter and carries more injury risk, especially for beginners. A hard set of 3 to 8 reps gives an estimate that is accurate enough to plan training.

Using the table

GoalLoadReps a set
Strength80 to 90%3 to 6
Muscle size65 to 80%6 to 12
Muscular endurance50 to 65%12 to 20

For general health, the WHO advises muscle-strengthening activity for all major muscle groups on at least 2 days a week. You do not need heavy barbells: body weight, bands and dumbbells all count, as long as the last few reps of a set feel hard.

Before you rely on this number

  • Accuracy drops above 10 reps and varies by lift and by person.
  • Use a set taken close to failure with good form. Sloppy reps inflate the estimate.
  • New to lifting, pregnant, or have a heart condition or high blood pressure? Get guidance before lifting heavy.

Questions people ask

How accurate are 1RM calculators?

Usually within about 5% when based on 2 to 8 hard reps. Treat the result as a planning number.

How often should I retest?

Every 6 to 8 weeks is plenty. If the same weight feels easier for the same reps, you are stronger.
