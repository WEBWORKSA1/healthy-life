A1C converter

Enter an A1C result to see the estimated average glucose in mg/dL and mmol/L, the IFCC value, and the range it falls in.

Your entries stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us.

The conversion

Estimated average glucose (mg/dL) = 28.7 × A1C − 46.7

To get mmol/L, divide by 18. The formula comes from a study that matched A1C results with months of continuous glucose readings.

A1CAverage glucose
5%97 mg/dL5.4 mmol/L
6%126 mg/dL7.0 mmol/L
7%154 mg/dL8.6 mmol/L
8%183 mg/dL10.2 mmol/L
9%212 mg/dL11.8 mmol/L

The ranges

A1CRange
Under 5.7%Normal
5.7 to 6.4%Prediabetes
6.5% or higherDiabetes

A diagnosis needs a second confirming test and a doctor's judgement. If you already have diabetes, many adults are given a target under 7%, but targets are personal.

Prediabetes is a fork in the road

Most people with prediabetes do not know they have it. Losing 5 to 7% of body weight and reaching 150 active minutes a week more than halved the rate of progression to type 2 diabetes in a landmark U.S. trial.

Before you rely on this number

  • A1C can read falsely high or low with anaemia, recent blood loss or transfusion, kidney disease, pregnancy and some haemoglobin variants.
  • Average glucose hides highs and lows. Two people with the same A1C can have very different daily patterns.
  • This converter does not diagnose. Discuss your result with the clinician who ordered the test.

Questions people ask

What is a normal A1C?

Under 5.7%.

How often should A1C be checked?

Typically every 3 to 6 months with diabetes, yearly with prediabetes, and every 3 years from age 35 if you are overweight and results are normal. Your doctor will advise.
