Caffeine cut-off calculator

Enter your bedtime and what you drink. See when to have your last one, how much caffeine is still in you at bedtime, and your daily total.

Your entries stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us.

How it works

Caffeine leaves the body slowly. Its half-life, the time to clear half a dose, is about 5 hours in most adults but ranges from under 3 to more than 9.

Amount left = dose × 0.5 raised to (hours since the drink ÷ half-life)

Example: a 95 mg coffee at 3 pm with a 5-hour half-life leaves about 31 mg at 11 pm.

We set your cut-off so that a single serving has fallen under 25 mg by bedtime, and never later than 8 hours before bed. In one well-known sleep-lab study, 400 mg taken even 6 hours before bed cut sleep by more than an hour, and people did not notice.

Typical amounts

DrinkCaffeine
Brewed coffee, 240 ml (8 oz)about 95 mg
Espresso, one shotabout 63 mg
Black tea, 240 mlabout 47 mg
Green tea, 240 mlabout 28 mg
Cola, 355 ml (12 oz)about 34 mg
Energy drink, 250 mlabout 80 mg

Coffee-shop servings are often two to three times a home cup.

Daily limits

The U.S. FDA cites 400 mg a day as an amount not generally associated with negative effects in healthy adults. In pregnancy, most guidance advises staying under 200 mg.

Before you rely on this number

  • Half-life is longer in pregnancy, with liver conditions and with some medicines (including some contraceptive pills), and shorter in smokers.
  • Sensitivity is partly genetic. If you sleep badly, move the cut-off earlier whatever the numbers say.
  • Children and teenagers should have far less; many paediatric bodies advise none for young children.

Questions people ask

How late can I drink coffee?

As a rule, at least 8 hours before bed. For an 11 pm bedtime that means a last coffee by about 3 pm, and earlier for large servings.

Does decaf have caffeine?

A little: usually 2 to 15 mg a cup.
