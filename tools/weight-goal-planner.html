Weight goal planner

Set a goal weight and a pace. The planner gives a daily calorie target, the time it should take and guard rails that keep the plan safe.

Your entries stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us.

How the plan is built

  1. We estimate the calories that hold your weight steady (TDEE).
  2. Your chosen pace sets a daily gap, using the rule of thumb that 1 kg of body weight is about 7,700 kcal (1 lb is about 3,500).
  3. Time to goal is the weight difference divided by the pace.
PaceDaily gap
0.25 kg (0.5 lb) a weekabout 275 kcal
0.5 kg (1 lb) a weekabout 550 kcal
0.75 kg (1.5 lb) a weekabout 825 kcal

The guard rails

  • Goals that would put your BMI under 18.5 are not accepted.
  • Daily targets never go under 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 kcal for men. If your pace would need less, the planner slows the pace instead.
  • A warning shows if the pace is more than 1% of your body weight a week.

What actually works

Slow, boring and sustainable beats fast. People who keep weight off tend to do the same few things: eat mostly minimally processed food, keep protein up, move daily, sleep enough and weigh in regularly. A 5 to 10% loss is enough to improve blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol.

Expect progress to slow. The 7,700 kcal rule overestimates loss over time because a smaller body burns less. Re-run the planner every few weeks.

Before you rely on this number

  • For adults who are not pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • If you have diabetes, take medicines affected by weight or diet, or have a BMI over 40, plan changes with your doctor.
  • If thoughts about food or weight are taking over your day, please talk to a doctor or an eating-disorder helpline rather than using this tool.

Questions people ask

How fast is it safe to lose weight?

Most guidelines advise 0.5 to 1 kg (1 to 2 lb) a week at most. This planner caps the pace at 0.75 kg.

Why did the planner slow my pace?

Your chosen pace would have needed fewer calories than the floor we use for safety, so it found the fastest pace that stays above it.
