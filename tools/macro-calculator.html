Macro calculator

Turn your calorie needs into grams of protein, carbohydrate and fat, with four eating styles to choose from.

Your entries stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us.

From calories to grams

  1. We estimate your daily calories (TDEE) and adjust for your goal: 15% under to lose slowly, 10% over to gain slowly.
  2. We split those calories by the percentages of your chosen style.
  3. We convert to grams: protein and carbohydrate have 4 kcal per gram, fat has 9.
StyleProteinCarbohydrateFat
Balanced25%45%30%
Higher protein30%40%30%
Lower carb30%30%40%
Endurance20%55%25%

Example: 2,000 kcal, balanced: 125 g protein, 225 g carbohydrate, 67 g fat.

Which style?

All four sit inside the ranges most nutrition bodies consider adequate. Pick the one closest to how you already like to eat; the diet you can keep up beats the "optimal" one you abandon. Whatever the split, quality counts for more than ratios: vegetables, fruit, whole grains, pulses, nuts, fish and minimally processed foods.

You do not need to hit it exactly

Treat the grams as a guide for the week, not a daily exam. Being within 10% is plenty.

Before you rely on this number

  • Targets below 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men) are not shown.
  • Not designed for pregnancy, breastfeeding, under-18s, kidney disease or diabetes management. Those need individual advice.
  • Counting macros is a tool, not a requirement for eating well. If tracking makes you anxious about food, stop.

Questions people ask

What are macros?

Macronutrients: protein, carbohydrate and fat, the three nutrients that supply energy.

What is the best macro split for weight loss?

Trials comparing low-fat and low-carb diets find similar results when calories and protein are matched. A higher-protein split helps many people feel full and keep muscle.
