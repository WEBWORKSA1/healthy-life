BMI calculator

Enter your height and weight to see your body mass index on the standard adult scale, with the weight range that counts as healthy for your height.

How BMI is worked out

Body mass index is your weight divided by the square of your height.

  • Metric: weight in kg ÷ (height in m × height in m)
  • US units: weight in lb ÷ (height in in × height in in) × 703

Example: 70 kg at 1.70 m is 70 ÷ (1.7 × 1.7) = 24.2.

What the categories mean

BMICategory for adults
Under 18.5Underweight
18.5 to 24.9Healthy weight
25.0 to 29.9Overweight
30.0 to 34.9Obesity, class 1
35.0 to 39.9Obesity, class 2
40.0 or moreObesity, class 3

These cut-offs are used by the CDC and the World Health Organization for adults aged 20 and over. They describe risk across large groups of people. For one person, BMI is a first look, not a diagnosis.

What to do with your number

If your BMI is outside the healthy band, the most useful next step is a second measurement. Your waist-to-height ratio shows whether weight is carried around the middle, which matters more for heart and metabolic risk than total weight.

Before you rely on this number

  • BMI cannot tell muscle from fat. Athletes and people who lift weights often score as overweight with low body fat.
  • It tends to underestimate body fat in older adults who have lost muscle.
  • Health risk starts at lower BMIs for people of South Asian, Chinese and some other backgrounds; some countries use 23 as the overweight threshold.
  • It is not for children, teenagers or pregnancy. Under 20, doctors use age- and sex-specific percentile charts.

Questions people ask

What is a healthy BMI?

For adults, 18.5 to 24.9 is classed as healthy weight. Where you feel and function best inside that range depends on build, muscle and age.

Is BMI accurate?

It is a reasonable screening tool for populations and a rough one for individuals. Pair it with waist-to-height ratio, blood pressure and blood tests for a fuller picture.

Does BMI differ for men and women?

The formula and adult cut-offs are the same. Women typically carry more body fat than men at the same BMI.
