The categories

Severely highOver 180and/orOver 120

These are the American Heart Association and American College of Cardiology categories. If your two numbers fall in different categories, the higher one counts. Many other countries, including the UK and much of Europe, diagnose high blood pressure from 140/90.

One reading is not a diagnosis

Blood pressure moves all day with stress, caffeine, talking, a full bladder and how you are sitting. Doctors diagnose from the average of several readings, often a week of home measurements.

What lowers it

Being active, losing a little weight if you carry extra, eating less salt and more vegetables and fruit, drinking less alcohol and not smoking each lower blood pressure by a few points. Together they can match a medicine. If you are prescribed medicine, lifestyle changes work alongside it, not instead of it.

Before you rely on this number

  • For adults who are not pregnant. In pregnancy, readings of 140/90 or higher need prompt medical review.
  • Wrist and finger monitors are less reliable than validated upper-arm cuffs.
  • This tool sorts a reading into a category. It cannot diagnose or rule out high blood pressure.

Questions people ask

What is a normal blood pressure?

Under 120/80 mm Hg for adults.

When is blood pressure an emergency?

A reading above 180/120 with chest pain, shortness of breath, back pain, numbness, weakness, vision change or difficulty speaking needs an emergency call. Without symptoms, re-measure after a minute and contact a health professional promptly if it stays that high.

Which number matters more?

Both. After age 50, a raised top number is the more common problem and a strong predictor of risk.
