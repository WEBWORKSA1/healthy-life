Healthy weight range

Enter your height to see the weight range that gives a healthy BMI. Add your current weight to see where you sit.

Your entries stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us.

Where the range comes from

The range is the set of weights that give a body mass index between 18.5 and 24.9 at your height: the band that the World Health Organization and the CDC class as healthy for adults.

Example: at 170 cm, BMI 18.5 is 53.5 kg and BMI 24.9 is 72 kg. At 5 ft 7 in, that is about 118 to 159 lb.

Why it is a range, not a number

Formulas that give one "ideal weight" were mostly designed to calculate drug doses, not to tell anyone what they should weigh. A span of roughly 18 kg (40 lb) is honest about how much healthy bodies differ in frame and muscle.

If you are outside it

A few kilos above the range with a waist under half your height, normal blood pressure and regular activity is a different situation from the same weight with a large waist. Check your waist-to-height ratio next. If you want to change your weight, the weight goal planner works out a pace that stays safe.

Before you rely on this number

  • Built on BMI, so it shares BMI's blind spots: muscle, age, ethnicity and body shape.
  • Not for under-20s or pregnancy.
  • A healthy weight for you may sit slightly outside the band. Your doctor can judge that with other measures.

Questions people ask

How much should I weigh for my height?

There is no single correct weight. The calculator shows the span linked with the lowest health risk for most adults of your height.

Is the range different for women and men?

The BMI band is the same. Men usually sit towards the upper part of it and women towards the middle, because of differences in muscle and frame.
