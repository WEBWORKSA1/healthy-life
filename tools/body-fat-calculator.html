Body fat calculator

No calipers or scanner needed. A tape measure around your neck, waist and (for women) hips gives a usable estimate of body fat.

Your entries stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us.

The method

The U.S. Navy formula predicts body fat from circumferences and height. It was developed on service personnel and checked against underwater weighing.

  • Men: uses waist minus neck, and height
  • Women: uses waist plus hips minus neck, and height

It works because waist and hip size rise with fat, while neck size tracks lean mass.

How to measure

  • Neck: just below the Adam's apple, tape sloping slightly down at the front.
  • Waist: men at the navel; women at the narrowest point. Breathe out normally.
  • Hips (women): around the widest part of the buttocks.

Take each measurement twice and use the average.

Reading the result

Category (ACE chart)WomenMen
Essential fat10–13%2–5%
Athletes14–20%6–13%
Fitness21–24%14–17%
Average25–31%18–24%
Above average32% and over25% and over

Body fat naturally rises with age, and women need more of it than men for normal hormone function. Very low levels are not a health goal.

Before you rely on this number

  • Expect an error of 3 to 4 percentage points either way compared with a DEXA scan.
  • Less accurate for very lean, very muscular or older bodies, and for unusual fat distribution.
  • Track the trend with the same tape and method rather than fixating on a single reading.

Questions people ask

How accurate is the Navy method?

Good enough to place you in a category and follow change over months. It is not precise enough to detect small week-to-week changes.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

Broadly, 21 to 31% for women and 14 to 24% for men cover the fitness and average bands on the ACE chart. Health depends on much more than this one figure.
