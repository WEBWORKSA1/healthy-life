Healthy.Life for teams

A simple wellbeing programme your people will use: one score, one challenge a month, and no app to install.

What a team programme includes

  • Kick-offEveryone takes the 3-minute Healthy Life Score privately. Nobody's answers are shared with the employer.
  • Monthly challengeWalking, sleep, hydration, calm or connection, with a team finish line and certificates.
  • Tool of the weekA short message with one calculator and one guide, ready to paste into your chat or newsletter.
  • Expert sessionsOptional talks from vetted dietitians, sleep and exercise professionals.
  • Provider matchingIf you want a full service, we introduce up to three corporate-wellness providers that fit your size and budget.

Why employers ask

Inactivity, poor sleep and stress show up as sick days, turnover and lost concentration. Programmes work when they are easy to join, respect privacy and are backed by managers. That is the design brief here.

Healthy.Life provides general wellbeing education. It is not occupational health, medical screening or an employee assistance programme, and it does not replace them.
