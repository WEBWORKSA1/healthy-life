Our standards

  • Every paid placement is labelled "Sponsored" or "Advertisement".
  • Sponsors have no say over calculations, content, sources or contest judging.
  • We do not accept advertising for tobacco or vaping, alcohol, gambling, weight-loss drugs or supplements making disease claims, crash diets, or anything promising a cure.
  • We do not share personal data with advertisers. Tool entries never leave the visitor's browser.
