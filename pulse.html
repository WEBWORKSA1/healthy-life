How to read health news

  • One study is one study. Guidelines change when many studies agree.
  • Association is not cause. "Linked with" means two things were seen together, not that one produced the other.
  • Check who was studied. Results in mice, or in 20 young men, may not apply to you.
  • Look for absolute numbers. "Doubles the risk" matters far more when the starting risk is 1 in 10 than 1 in 10,000.
