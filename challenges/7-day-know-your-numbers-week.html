Prevent challenge

Know-your-numbers week

Most people do not know their blood pressure, waist measurement or resting heart rate. This week you collect one number a day, using a tape measure, a clock and the free tools on this site.

Your progress is saved in this browser only.

How it works

  • One number a day. Each takes under ten minutes.
  • Everything is saved to My numbers in this browser.
  • Numbers are for awareness. Only a health professional can diagnose.
  • A blood pressure reading above 180/120, especially with symptoms, needs urgent medical attention.

Day by day

  1. Day 1 Measure your height and weight and note your BMI.
  2. Day 2 Measure your waist and work out your waist-to-height ratio.
  3. Day 3 Take your resting heart rate first thing in the morning.
  4. Day 4 Get a blood pressure reading at home or at a pharmacy.
  5. Day 5 Add up your active minutes for the past week.
  6. Day 6 Count your alcoholic drinks and your caffeine for a typical week.
  7. Day 7 Check which screenings are due at your age, and book anything overdue.

Tools that help

  • BMI calculatorYour body mass index, its category and your healthy weight range.
  • Waist-to-height ratioWhether your waist is under half your height, the simplest check of risky belly fat.
  • Heart-rate zone calculatorYour five training zones in beats per minute.
  • Blood pressure checkerWhat your reading means, by the American Heart Association categories.
  • Weekly activity checkerDo you reach the 150-minute guideline? Find out in three entries.
  • Alcohol calculatorYour weekly alcohol in grams, units and standard drinks.
  • Checkup checklistThe screenings and checks to ask about at your age.

Questions people ask

I don't have a blood pressure monitor.

Many pharmacies offer free checks, and a validated upper-arm home monitor is inexpensive. Sit quietly for five minutes first.

What do I do with the numbers?

Open My numbers to see them together, and take the list to your next checkup. Trends over time matter more than any single reading.
