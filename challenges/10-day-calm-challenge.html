Mind challenge

10-day calm challenge

Stress is not the enemy; having no way to come down from it is. Each day you try one small, well-studied practice. By day ten you will know which two or three work for you.

Your progress is saved in this browser only.

How it works

  • Each practice takes 2 to 10 minutes.
  • Do it at the same time each day if you can; after lunch works well.
  • You are testing what suits you. Not every practice will.
  • If low mood or anxiety is getting in the way of daily life, or you feel unsafe, talk to a doctor or a local helpline. This challenge is not treatment.

Day by day

  1. Day 1 Two minutes of slow breathing: in for 4, out for 6.
  2. Day 2 A 10-minute walk outdoors without headphones. Notice five things you can see.
  3. Day 3 Write down what is on your mind for 5 minutes, then close the notebook.
  4. Day 4 Box breathing for 3 minutes: in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4.
  5. Day 5 Tense and release each muscle group from feet to face, 5 minutes.
  6. Day 6 One phone-free hour this evening.
  7. Day 7 Write three specific things that went well today.
  8. Day 8 Do one task with full attention: tea, a shower, washing up.
  9. Day 9 Tell someone one thing you appreciate about them.
  10. Day 10 Choose your two favourite practices and schedule them for next week.

Tools that help

  • Breathing timerA guided circle for box breathing and three other calming patterns.
  • Sleep calculatorThe best time to go to bed or wake up, counted in sleep cycles.

Questions people ask

Does breathing really change stress?

Slow breathing with a longer out-breath nudges the nervous system towards its rest state and lowers heart rate. Trials show small to moderate reductions in self-reported stress and anxiety.

I can't sit still to meditate. Is that a problem?

No. Walking, stretching and focused chores count. The skill is returning your attention, not sitting motionless.
