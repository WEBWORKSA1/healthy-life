30-day walking challenge

Thirty days, one walk a day. You start with 10 minutes and add 5 each week, so by the final week you are covering the 150 minutes of moderate activity that the WHO recommends for adults.

Your progress is saved in this browser only.

How it works

  • One walk a day. Split it in two if that fits your day better.
  • The right pace is brisk: you can talk, but you could not sing.
  • Missed a day? Carry on with today's walk. Do not double up.
  • Sore joints or chest discomfort are a signal to stop and check with a doctor.

Day by day

  1. Day 1 Walk 10 minutes at a pace where you can talk but not sing.

Questions people ask

Do I need 10,000 steps?

No. 10,000 began as a marketing number. Studies link benefits to totals well below it, and the guideline is written in minutes, not steps: 150 or more a week at moderate effort.

Can I walk on a treadmill?

Yes. Set a 1% incline to roughly match outdoor effort.

What if 10 minutes is too much?

Start with 5 minutes twice a day and repeat week one until it feels comfortable.
