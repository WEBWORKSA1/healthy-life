Sleep challenge

14-day sleep reset

Most sleep trouble that is not medical comes down to timing, light and wind-down. This reset changes one thing each day and keeps everything you have already added.

Your progress is saved in this browser only.

How it works

  • Each day adds one habit. Keep the earlier ones going.
  • The single most important rule is the fixed wake-up time. Hold it at weekends too.
  • If you cannot sleep after about 20 minutes, get up, do something quiet in low light and return when sleepy.
  • Loud snoring with pauses, or weeks of poor sleep despite good habits, deserves a doctor's visit.

Day by day

  1. Day 1 Choose your wake-up time. Set the alarm for it every day this fortnight.
  2. Day 2 Get outside for 10 minutes of daylight within an hour of waking.
  3. Day 3 Work out your caffeine cut-off time and stick to it.
  4. Day 4 Count back from your wake-up time to set a bedtime that allows 7 to 9 hours.
  5. Day 5 Dim the lights and screens for the last hour of the evening.
  6. Day 6 Charge your phone outside the bedroom.
  7. Day 7 Make the room darker: cover LEDs, close the gaps in the curtains.
  8. Day 8 Cool the bedroom to around 18°C (65°F), or open a window.
  9. Day 9 Build a 20-minute wind-down in the same order every night.
  10. Day 10 Finish alcohol at least 3 hours before bed, or skip it.
  11. Day 11 Finish your last big meal 3 hours before bed.
  12. Day 12 Move your body today, but finish hard exercise 3 hours before bed.
  13. Day 13 Write tomorrow's to-do list before your wind-down, then close the notebook.
  14. Day 14 Review: which three changes helped most? Those are your keepers.

Tools that help

  • Caffeine cut-off calculatorThe latest time for coffee if you want to sleep well tonight.
  • Breathing timerA guided circle for box breathing and three other calming patterns.

Questions people ask

I work shifts. Can I do this?

Yes, but anchor to your main sleep period rather than the clock: light on waking, dark and cool for sleep, a steady routine on each run of shifts.

Should I use a sleep tracker?

Optional. How rested you feel during the day is the better measure. If tracking makes you anxious about sleep, leave it off.
