Connect challenge

7-day reach-out challenge

Strong relationships are linked with longer, healthier lives, and they are built from small, repeated contact. Each task here takes minutes.

Your progress is saved in this browser only.

How it works

  • Keep it small. A two-line message counts.
  • Aim for real exchanges, not likes or reactions.
  • If someone does not reply, that is about their week, not about you.
  • Feeling persistently lonely is common and nothing to be ashamed of. A doctor or community organisation can point you to local groups.

Day by day

  1. Day 1 Message someone you have not spoken to in months. Say what made you think of them.
  2. Day 2 Have one conversation today with no phone in your hand.
  3. Day 3 Thank someone and say exactly what for.
  4. Day 4 Ask a neighbour, colleague or shop assistant a real question, and listen.
  5. Day 5 Call a relative or old friend for ten minutes.
  6. Day 6 Invite someone to do something this week: a walk, a coffee, a meal.
  7. Day 7 Put one recurring catch-up in your calendar.

Questions people ask

I'm an introvert. Do I need lots of friends?

No. The research points to the quality and reliability of a few relationships, not the number.

Does online contact count?

A video call or a long message thread with someone you know counts. Passive scrolling does not have the same effect.
