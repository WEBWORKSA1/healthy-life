Nourish challenge

21-day hydration challenge

Week one attaches water to things you already do. Week two makes it easier to reach. Week three hits your full target every day. Work out your target first with the water intake calculator.

Your progress is saved in this browser only.

How it works

  • Find your target with the water intake calculator before you begin.
  • All unsweetened drinks count. Tea and coffee count too.
  • Spread drinks through the day rather than catching up at night.
  • If you have been told to limit fluids for a heart or kidney condition, follow that advice instead.

Day by day

  1. Day 1 Drink a glass of water within 30 minutes of waking.
  2. Day 2 Morning glass, plus one with lunch.
  3. Day 3 Morning and lunch glasses, plus one mid-afternoon.
  4. Day 4 Three glasses as before. Carry a bottle today.
  5. Day 5 Swap one sugary or diet drink for water.
  6. Day 6 Have a glass before each meal.
  7. Day 7 Count your drinks today. How close to your target are you?
  8. Day 8 Fill a bottle in the morning and finish it by lunch.
  9. Day 9 Finish one bottle by lunch and one by dinner.
  10. Day 10 Add a slice of lemon, cucumber or mint to keep it interesting.
  11. Day 11 Drink a glass before and after any exercise.
  12. Day 12 Swap a second sweet drink for sparkling water or unsweetened tea.
  13. Day 13 Check the colour: pale yellow means you are on track.
  14. Day 14 Hit your full daily target once this week. Today is a good day.
  15. Day 15 Full target today. Tick it off before dinner.
  16. Day 16 Full target, with most of it before 6 pm so sleep is not disturbed.
  17. Day 17 Full target. Notice your energy mid-afternoon.
  18. Day 18 Full target. Eat two water-rich foods: fruit, soup, salad or yoghurt.
  19. Day 19 Full target, even though it is the weekend.
  20. Day 20 Full target. Decide which two cues you will keep.
  21. Day 21 Final day: full target. Your new baseline is set.

Tools that help

  • Water intake calculatorHow much to drink a day, adjusted for exercise and heat.
  • Caffeine cut-off calculatorThe latest time for coffee if you want to sleep well tonight.

Questions people ask

Is eight glasses a day the rule?

It is a fair rough guide but not a requirement. Needs vary with body size, activity and heat. Thirst and pale-yellow urine are reliable signals for most healthy adults.

Can I drink too much?

Yes, though it is rare. Drinking several litres in a short time can dilute blood sodium. Spread your intake and do not force it far beyond thirst.
