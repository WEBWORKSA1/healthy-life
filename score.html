Healthy Life Score

How do your everyday habits compare with what the guidelines recommend? Find out in three minutes.

What the score measures

PillarQuestions coverBenchmarks used
MoveWeekly activity, strength days, sitting timeWHO physical activity guidelines
NourishVegetables and fruit, sugary drinks, ready-made mealsWHO healthy diet advice
SleepHours, regular timing, feeling restedAmerican Academy of Sleep Medicine
MindStress load, ways to unwind, daylightNHS and NIH wellbeing guidance
ConnectConversations, support, sense of purposeWHO Commission on Social Connection
PreventTobacco, alcohol, checkupsCDC, WHO and national alcohol guidance

What the bands mean

  • 85 to 100, Thriving: habits match the guidelines almost everywhere.
  • 70 to 84, Strong: good foundations, one or two pillars to work on.
  • 50 to 69, Building: a mix of strengths and gaps.
  • Under 50, Starting: the most room to gain, and the fastest early wins.

The score is a mirror, not a grade. Circumstances, health conditions and caring duties shape what is possible, and the scoring cannot see them.

Questions people ask

How is the Healthy Life Score calculated?

Each of the 18 answers earns 0 to 100 points, set by how closely it matches published guidelines. The three answers in each pillar are averaged, and the six pillar scores are averaged into your total.

Is this a medical test?

No. It is a self-check of everyday habits. It does not measure disease risk, and it cannot diagnose or rule out any condition.

Where do my answers go?

Nowhere. They are stored in your browser so that you can see your trend. We do not receive them.

How often should I retake it?

Once a month is enough to see whether changes are sticking.

The Healthy Life Score is an educational self-assessment. It is not a medical device, a health-risk calculator or a substitute for professional advice.
