Questions people ask

Is the plan really free?

Yes. The first week shows before you give any details. The other three weeks open as soon as you add a name and email.

What happens if I ask for an introduction?

We pass your goals, focus pillars, preferred format and contact details to up to three professionals or programmes that fit. They contact you directly. There is no obligation, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Do you sell my data?

No. Your details go only to the matches you asked for. If you do not tick the introduction box, nobody else receives them.

Do you ask about medical conditions?

No. The plan covers general lifestyle steps. Anything medical belongs with your own doctor.

The plan is general wellbeing guidance for adults. It is not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or take regular medicines, check changes with your doctor first.
