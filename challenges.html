How challenges work

  1. Open a challenge and read the day-by-day list.
  2. Tap each day when you have done it. Your progress stays in this browser.
  3. Missed a day? Carry on. Research on habit formation shows a missed day makes no measurable difference.
  4. Finish every day to download a certificate, then enter the monthly contest.

Which one first?

Take the Healthy Life Score. Your lowest pillar tells you where a challenge will pay back most. If you are unsure, the 30-day walking challenge and the 14-day sleep reset help almost everyone.
